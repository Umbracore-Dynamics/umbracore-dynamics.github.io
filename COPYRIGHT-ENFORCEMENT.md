## Protected materials

This policy applies to the Umbracore Dynamics website and repository, including its source code, layout, interaction design, visual system, charts, animations, written content, service descriptions, documentation, logo, brand identity, policies, schemas, and technical architecture.

## Prohibited uses

Without prior written authorization from Umbracore Dynamics, no person or organization may:

- Clone, mirror, reproduce, republish, or redistribute the website.
- Copy or substantially imitate its visual design, interface, animations, navigation, chart presentation, or interactive behavior.
- Create derivative websites, templates, products, or services from the protected materials.
- Remove or alter copyright, trademark, attribution, or legal notices.
- Use the source code, content, screenshots, design, or documentation for commercial or competitive purposes.
- Use the materials to train, fine-tune, benchmark, evaluate, or develop automated or artificial-intelligence systems.
- Present copied material as original work or imply affiliation with Umbracore Dynamics.
- Register confusingly similar domains, accounts, products, or branding.

## Public repository limitation

Public visibility, browser delivery, cloning, or forking through GitHub does not grant permission to reuse, distribute, adapt, commercialize, or create derivative works. Rights are granted only through an explicit written agreement signed by an authorized representative of Umbracore Dynamics.

## Evidence preservation

Umbracore Dynamics may preserve the following evidence when investigating suspected infringement:

- Repository commit history and cryptographic commit identifiers.
- Publication and deployment timestamps.
- Archived website captures and screenshots.
- Source-file hashes.
- Domain-registration and hosting records.
- Similarity comparisons involving code, layout, content, graphics, and behavior.
- Communications and licensing records.

## Enforcement measures

Depending on the circumstances and applicable law, Umbracore Dynamics may:

1. Send a direct cease-and-desist or licensing request.
2. Submit a copyright or trademark complaint to the relevant hosting, domain, social-media, advertising, or platform provider.
3. Request removal from search engines or content-distribution services.
4. Preserve evidence for legal counsel or regulatory authorities.
5. Seek injunctive relief, damages, costs, or other remedies available under applicable law.

## Good-faith reports

Reports of suspected copying should include:

- The URL or repository containing the suspected copy.
- Screenshots or archived evidence.
- The date the material was observed.
- A brief explanation of the similarities.
- The reporter’s contact details.

Send reports to:

`umbracore.solutions@outlook.com`

Use the subject:

`COPYRIGHT REPORT — Umbracore Dynamics`

## Permission requests

Requests for authorized reuse, licensing, partnership, or publication must be submitted in writing to the same address. Silence, public availability, or technical access does not constitute permission.
